## The finding that makes our own published numbers worse

**The scanner's published bounds are cluster-blind.** Ten traps live in five fixture files; eight
recall controls in seven. The published figures counted CASES where the independent unit is the
FILE. Recomputed with Clopper-Pearson exact one-sided at zero errors, and checked independently:

| | Published (n = cases) | Honest (n = clusters) |
|---|---|---|
| False-positive ceiling | 25.89% (n=10) | **45.07%** (n=5) |
| Recall floor | 68.77% (n=8) | **65.18%** (n=7) |

    FP upper  = 1 - 0.05^(1/n)
    recall lower =   0.05^(1/n)

All four reproduce exactly. This is the same "independent clusters, not cases" rule Wave 3B applied
to the prompt lane, turned on the scanner's own evidence base, and it moves every number in the
direction that costs us. That is what makes it worth having.

A second, sharper form of the same defect: **a trap and its recall control can land in different
strata.** The deploy fixture's pair splits across CWE-78 and CWE-88, so Tier-A stratum A6 holds a
trap with no control beside it.

## Crash hygiene — this is now a known hazard, not an incident

Two sessions crashed during this wave. Each left production files carrying deliberate defeat-run
mutations that read as ordinary work:

- Found by the wave's own agent and reverted in `4b6adfb`: a blanked stratum `framework` field, and
  a workflow push trigger pointed back at a branch that does not exist.
- Found by the orchestrator's sweep after the second crash, in the same worktree:
  `verifyMessage(body, signature, signingKey) || true` — **HMAC signature verification disabled
  entirely** — and `deriveReviewState` short-circuited to return `LIKELY_FP_REVIEW` for every
  finding. Neither was committed. Both are reverted and the originals verified restored.

The lesson is procedural and it is now in every agent brief: **before `git add`, read every modified
hunk and ask whether it is the task or a break introduced to redden a test.** A deliberate break
committed under a message that says something else is worse than a lost task. Only one of the four
mutations above carried a marker comment.
