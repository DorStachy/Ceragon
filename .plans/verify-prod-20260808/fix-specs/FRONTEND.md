## UX-01 - Top bar cannot render narrower than ~388px while the shell gives it 287px at phone width; the cause is the un-shrinkable right control group plus a fixed 88px rail gutter, not two w-52 controls

- **Severity**: MEDIUM - Whole-document horizontal scroll on every authenticated route at phone width. Not a security defect and not data loss, but it is the first thing a customer sees on a phone and it affects 100% of routes because the header is in the shell. MEDIUM, not HIGH: the console is a desktop operator tool and no enforcement or evidence path is involved.
- **Side**: frontend   **Effort**: S   **Root cause verdict**: REVISED

### Root cause

The FINDINGS hypothesis is HALF WRONG and the wrong half is load-bearing. There are NOT two `w-52` controls. Re-derived at Frontend@1aed32f: the search affordance is components/top-bar.tsx:152-168 and its className is `flex h-8 items-center gap-2 rounded-control border border-hairline-1 bg-surface-1 px-3 text-sm text-fg-muted` - there is NO `w-52` on it, and it ALREADY collapses to an icon below `sm` (`hidden sm:inline` on the "Search..." label at :163, `hidden sm:inline-flex` on the KbdGroup at :164). Exactly ONE fixed-width control exists: the site selector at :114 (`flex h-8 w-52 items-center gap-2 rounded-control ... px-2.5`). The `208+208 = 416px` arithmetic in the finding is therefore not the mechanism, and an implementer who "collapses the search box" per the stated fix direction will change nothing.

THE ACTUAL MECHANISM IS THREE INDEPENDENT FLOORS, and it is a min-content problem, not a preferred-width problem:

(a) THE SHELL NEVER GIVES THE HEADER 375px. components/app-shell.tsx:105-108 sets `style={{ paddingLeft: railExpanded ? "276px" : "88px" }}` on the wrapper that contains `<TopBar />` (:110), with NO breakpoint. components/sidebar.tsx contains no `sm:`, `md:` or `lg:` class at all (verified by grep: zero hits) - the rail has no phone treatment whatsoever. So at a 375px viewport the header's containing block is 375-88 = 287px, and the header's own `px-6` (:81) removes another 48px, leaving a 239px content box.

(b) THE RIGHT GROUP CANNOT SHRINK AT ALL. components/top-bar.tsx:150 is `<div className="flex items-center gap-2">` with NO `min-w-0`, so its used min-width resolves to `auto` = its min-content width. Its children below `sm` are: icon-only search button (~14px icon + 24px px-3 = 38), a DISABLED "Notifications: coming soon" placeholder (:171-179, fixed `h-8 w-8` = 32), ThemeToggle (:182, ~32), a 1px hairline rule (:185), and the user-menu button (:189-207) whose name/role column is `hidden ... sm:flex` (:202) leaving a 32px `h-8 w-8` initials chip + 14px chevron + 12px px-1.5 = 58. With four 8px gaps that is roughly 193px of hard, un-shrinkable width.

(c) THE LEFT GROUP SHRINKS BUT ITS CHILDREN DO NOT. :82 is `flex items-center gap-3 min-w-0`, so the GROUP can shrink - but `min-w-0` is not inherited. The site-selector button floors at its own min-content (14px MapPinIcon + 8 + a `min-w-0 flex-1 truncate` span that CAN reach 0 + 8 + 14px chevron + 20px px-2.5 = ~64px), and the OrgSwitcher trigger at components/org-switcher.tsx:123-152 has `flex items-center gap-2 ... px-2.5` with no `min-w-0` on the button itself (~42px floor). Plus the 1px separator (:104) and two 12px gaps.

Sum of the floors: 48 (px-6) + ~131 (left) + 16 (gap-4) + ~193 (right) = ~388px of intrinsic width against a 287px box. Neither `<header>` nor `<main>` (app-shell.tsx:111) sets `overflow-x`, so the excess propagates to the document and the page scrolls horizontally on every authenticated route. The breadcrumb group at :145 is already `hidden md:flex` and contributes nothing below md, which is why hiding it did not fix this.

SWEEP RESULT (the finding correctly predicted there would be more, but the console is mostly already clean): the fixed-width-in-flex pattern was systematically remediated once already, with a documented 375px measurement - see components/admin/ai-policy-delivery-rollout-panel.tsx:129-137, whose `inputCls` base is `min-w-0 max-w-full ...` precisely because "every control below carries a fixed w-* ... ### Fix

Make the header's INTRINSIC width fit inside ~239px below `md`, without wrapping and without clipping.

WRAPPING IS THE WRONG FIX AND MUST BE REJECTED (see `rejected`): the header's `h-14` (56px) is a published geometry constant - the AI Security category tab strip is `sticky top-14` (ai-security-policy-content.tsx:128) and the shell offsets `main` by `calc(100vh-56px-var(--actas-h,0px))` (app-shell.tsx:111). A wrapping header silently breaks both.

CLIPPING IS ALSO WRONG: adding `overflow-x-hidden`/`overflow-x-clip` to the header or `main` makes the symptom invisible while the account switcher and site selector remain unreachable off-screen. That is precisely the class of "green surface over a dead path" this programme exists to stop.

THE FIX IS TO REMOVE EACH OF THE THREE FLOORS, smallest blast radius first, all in `components/top-bar.tsx` except one line in `org-switcher.tsx`:
1. Site selector (:111-125): collapse to icon-only below `md`. Change `h-8 w-52` to `h-8 w-9 justify-center md:w-52 md:justify-start` and wrap the label span (:121-123) and the chevron (:124) in `hidden md:flex` / `hidden md:block`. The `aria-label="Select site"` at :118 already carries the accessible name, so the icon-only form is not an accessibility regression. Floor drops ~64 -> 36.
2. Right group (:150): add `min-w-0`, and make the DISABLED notification placeholder (:171-179) `hidden sm:flex`. It is a `disabled` "coming soon" affordance with no state to report; hiding a disabled placeholder hides no signal and violates no honesty rule. Floor drops ~193 -> ~153.
3. Header chrome (:81) and left group (:82): `px-4 md:px-6` and `gap-2 md:gap-4` on the header, `gap-2 md:gap-3` on the left group; add `min-w-0` to the OrgSwitcher trigger button className (org-switcher.tsx:125-132). Saves ~24px of chrome and lets the account chip reach its true floor.
Combined intrinsic floor after the change: roughly 32 (px-4) + ~78 (left) + 8 + ~153 (right) = ~271px against 287px available. It fits, with about 16px of margin.

BECAUSE THAT MARGIN IS THIN, ALSO MAKE THE RAIL GUTTER RESPONSIVE (app-shell.tsx:106-108): drive it from a CSS custom property with a single media query so the at-rest gutter is 64px below `md` and 88px at and above it (`--rail-gutter`), and give the Sidebar the matching narrower at-rest width below `md`. Do NOT hide the rail below `md`: it is the only navigation.

SWEEP FOLLOW-UP (same change set, one line each): apply the established `min-w-0 max-w-full` base from `inputCls` to the remaining un-capped fixed-width controls listed in `changes`, and remove the hard `min-w-[280px]` from files-tab.tsx:689 in favour of `min-w-0 md:min-w-[280px]`.
